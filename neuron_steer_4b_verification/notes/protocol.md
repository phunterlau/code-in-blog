# Frozen study: zero, egg and verification controls

The study uses the pinned Qwen3.5-4B checkpoint and existing audited hybrid runtime on lp. Transformer layers run in FP16 on CUDA; the tied vocabulary embedding/output boundary runs on CPU in FP32. The fixed behavior-conditioned score is the signed arithmetic mean of coupling times paired target-minus-base activation change, ranked by descending signed value. RMS is retained as a descriptive rank diagnostic only. No gradients are used.

Twenty-four authored discovery pairs cover zero-valued arithmetic, egg-related completions, and verification verbs. All rankings and full discovery tensors are sealed before the program reads reference coordinates. The investigator has seen the published examples; this is coordinate-excluded selection, not a claim of investigator blindness. Published candidates L26 N4405, L23 N7748 and L22 N4114 are credited to Neuron Explorer's Qwen3.5-4B source notes and caches at 0f3484031bccb061081207559183517942bccc76.

The causal screen tests each task's top-four positive PP scores and the published candidate on eight signed relative doses, alongside a seed-fixed uniform sample of 32 neurons screened at ±0.4. Each dose receives the same two target probes and four protection probes. The three calibration cohorts are the PP screen winner, published candidate and random-sweep winner. These may coincide; overlap is reported rather than relabeled as independent discoveries.

Generation calibration uses the best screen dose and its half under continuous, pulse4, and prefill-only schedules. Prediction index zero is the whole prefill. All schedules edit every prefill position; pulse4 edits predictions zero through three and then releases, while prefill-only releases after zero. No edit is a valid selection and wins ties in target performance and accuracy. Eligibility requires preservation of every baseline-correct calibration target and protection answer, no capped target responses, and a joint target success. A failed nonzero challenger is retained separately for diagnosis. All selections are sealed before evaluating eight new target cases and eight new protection cases per task.

Zero tasks balance four zero and four nonzero answers, including draft-answer distractions. Increased zero probability alone is not an accuracy gain. Egg tasks require the specified word and a simple coherence screen; final prose also needs manual inspection. Verification tasks balance correct and incorrect numerical claims, with the correct final answer measured independently from verification vocabulary. More verification words do not establish better reasoning. Common protections include arithmetic, exact identifier copying and a vegan ingredient constraint.

The theoretical maximum is below the 20,000-forward cap; the wall limit is 3,000 seconds. The run contains no backward passes. Raw token IDs, actual FP16 activation writes, source/protocol hashes, discovery/candidate/selection commitments, runtime device assertions and PID-correlated CUDA telemetry are retained. Prompt pairs, test outcomes and external inventories are not changed or used to reselect candidates after launch. New-candidate novelty checks happen offline after selection and are limited to the checked sources.
