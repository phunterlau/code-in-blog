     Method 
     
       
         Setup 
         h_L  ←  h_L + c · W_down[:, n]
c    =  ± α · r_L / ‖W_down[:, n]‖
α    ∈  {1.25, 1.0, 0.75, 0.5} 
         
           Model Qwen3-8B, 36 layers × 12,288 MLP neurons 
           r_L Median residual-stream norm after layer L over the prompt’s tokens (first token excluded) 
           Push 
           Prompt “Who are you?” in the chat template, thinking off 
           Unpushed “I am Qwen, a large language model developed by Alibaba Cloud. I can assist with a wide range of tasks, including answering questions, creating content, and providing information. How can I help you today?” 
           Decoding Greedy, up to 160 tokens. Stops early if the reply ends or repeats exactly. 
           Strength Starts at 1.25×. If the reply is incoherent, retries at 1.0×, 0.75×, then 0.5×. Keeps the strongest coherent reply. 
           Scale 884,736 neuron–direction pairs. 1,891,603 replies, counting retries. 
           Judge Claude Haiku 5.5 sees the reply and how the model normally answers. It gives a category, a persona name, a description, tags, weirdness (0–10) and coherence (0–10). 
         
       
       
         Limitations 
         
           One prompt.  A neuron may act differently on prompts other than “Who are you?”. 
           Not exactly reproducible.  Under strong pushes, about a third of replies change when only the batch changes. Of 580 clear personas rerun in three batch layouts, 536 reproduced. 
           Labels come from Claude Haiku.  No human has checked them. 
           Some replies are shortened.  Library cards show the first 220 characters. Neuron pages cut normal-Qwen replies at 280 characters and repeating replies at 480.
