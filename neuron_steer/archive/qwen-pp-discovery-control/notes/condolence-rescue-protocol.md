# Secondary experiment: dose and timing rescue of L22/N4945

This separate follow-up was frozen after the main calibration results and before any follow-up model output. Signed PP and RMS both selected L22/N4945. At continuous additive g=+1, it produced sympathy/apology markers and the requested word on both calibration prompts, but changed 7+4 from 11 to 10. That motivates a bounded dose/timing test, rather than treating the initial failure as proof that no useful intervention exists.

Coordinates: L22/N4945; public L10/N6295; and the signed method's random-screen winner L31/N5385. For each, test g=+0.2,+0.4,+0.6,+0.8,+1 with three schedules: all forwards, only the first four token predictions, or only predictions after those first four. Active forwards edit all their positions. The ledger records every active forward index for independent timing verification. The positive polarity is fixed from the motivating intervention; this is not an exhaustive null-direction benchmark.

Two new positive-news prompts and two new protected tasks select recipes. Both target examples must have an apology/sympathy marker, retain the specified word and avoid the repetition flag; both baseline-correct protections must remain correct. Among eligible recipes, prefer lower mean write norm per forward. A failed calibration selects a zero deployment fallback and a separately labeled diagnostic challenger.

Freeze selections before eight new positive-news prompts and four new protections. Also generate four loss-context and four neutral-context examples to inspect appropriate sympathy versus spillover. These diagnostics do not change selection. Continuous +1 on the candidate and public coordinate are predeclared comparison arms. The lexical effect on positive news is an overreach endpoint, not a claim of empathy improvement or full semantic correctness. Caps, repeated wording, fabricated premises and awkward language remain visible in raw text.

Maximum 9,000 forwards, 60 minutes, no backwards, no further outcome-driven expansion in this batch. The job starts after the CAPS generation audit exits successfully. Preserve all doses, failed gates, exact outputs, hashes, PIDs and telemetry.
