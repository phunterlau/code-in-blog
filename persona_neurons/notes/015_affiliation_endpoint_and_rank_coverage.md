# Affiliation endpoint correction and broader rank coverage

User clarification: a single target affiliation is unnecessary; mixed affiliations are acceptable. This supersedes the narrow interpretation of the earlier outcome, not the saved raw results or historical selection rule.

## Revised interpretation

Primary outcome: intervention-induced change in asserted self-affiliation, allowing any organization, a combination of organizations, or a fictional organization. Count affirmative self-attribution, not incidental mentions or quoted examples. Track model name, developer, ownership and association separately. A fluent claim such as Meta being Google-owned is a changed affiliation, not automatically a coherence failure just because it is factually false. Otherwise the intended OpenAI claim would itself be disallowed. Across-prompt variation among Samsung, DeepSeek and Hugging Face supports affiliation sensitivity; it does not invalidate the endpoint.

Report separate axes: changed attribution relative to the paired baseline; repetition/readability/contradiction; and unrelated task accuracy. Repeated tokens and explicit self-negation remain real failures, but mixed makers alone are not. An isolated factual control error is a measured side effect, not an automatic veto of affiliation steering. The earlier no-qualified-dose conclusion applies to the stricter frozen rule, not this newly clarified objective. Reanalysis under this objective is retrospective; freeze it before the next experiment.

## Why rank 565 can steer

Qwen3 L24:386: signed scenario activation differences 1.48047, 1.68555, 2.16357, 1.33203, 1.90039, 2.36719; mean +1.821533. First-token OpenAI-versus-Alibaba write/readout coupling -0.0775947. Signed-mean score -0.141341; absolute rank 565/350208 (top 0.161%). Activation-only rank 2640. No sign cancellation across these six scenario means explains this rank.

The successful additive dose is -98, magnitude 53.80 times the discovery mean difference and opposite its sign. The algebraic direct-write proxy c*t is +7.6043, versus c*mean_delta=-0.14134 in discovery. This is NOT a measured final-logit shift: normalization, downstream processing, different prompts/positions and autoregressive feedback intervene. The ratio is not an activation-standard-deviation or coherence-budget normalization. Thus modest discovery salience is compatible with a large finite-dose behavioral effect. Neither the finite-dose explanation nor an off-axis mechanism is established causally yet.

Paper consulted: https://arxiv.org/html/2606.19831v1 (Sections 2, 3.4, 5, 7). It separates geometric leverage from coherent behavioral reach and proposes residual/write-norm-normalized dosing with sign-specific windows and rollout horizons. Our fixed unembedding coupling is not the end-to-end gradient studied there; the connection is a hypothesis to test, not a proved equivalence.

## Proposed next experiment; not launched

1. Freeze broad affiliation-change labels and separate quality axes. Use paired baseline responses; distinguish changed but noncanonical attributions from new organization mentions. Calibrate self-grading on mixed-but-fluent affiliations, explicit contradictions, and repetition; failed rubric scores remain diagnostic.
2. Cover all top 1000 PP coordinates for the selected identity observable, rather than only the top three. This includes 565 but is a retrospectively chosen benchmark-coverage cutoff, not blind recovery. Add layer-stratified candidates from ranks 1001-5000, an activation-contrast-only pool without multiplying by readout coupling, and uniform random controls. Keep signed means; use separate contrasts for name, developer and association rather than pooling opposing entities before ranking. Candidate pools must be frozen before outcomes.
3. Measure residual norms, write-column norms and baseline residual/write alignment on neutral calibration prompts and generation positions. Define B=norm(residual)/norm(write) and g=dose/B. Match controls on g, layer and position; keep the reference raw dose -98 as a benchmark. Calibrate on the actual checkpoint/precision, not borrowed family constants. Preserve the baseline B convention for the dose sweep and log realized alignment along edited rollouts.
4. Initial proposed grid: both signs of g=0.10, 0.30, 0.60; expand coherently unchanged cases toward a locally calibrated ceiling and refine observed transitions. Do not assume monotonic behavior or symmetry. Track results separately at 32 and 128 tokens; short-horizon negatives must have a reserved longer-rollout audit to estimate missed late effects. Six signed doses times two prompts times 1000 candidates already means 12000 exploratory responses, before expanded pools. Benchmark actual throughput before estimating GPU hours.
5. Compare equal-compute PP-only, activation-only and stratified-union candidate pools. Treat known L24:386 as a benchmark excluded from novel-discovery yield. Report yield by rank band, dose-response curves, and the dose interval of coherent affiliation change, rather than maximum chosen-example success.
6. Freeze candidate/dose choices and test on untouched prompts with paired controls, independent decoding seeds where sampling is used, and both short introductions and contextual questions. Report side-effect rates separately. Examine prefill-only, decode-only and all-position dosing on finalists to test whether continued forcing is necessary.

Decisive test: if rank-565-like neurons repeatedly have useful normalized-dose windows while the top-ranked candidates collapse or remain affiliation-inert, the discovery ranking is a recall heuristic rather than a ranking of controllability. If its advantage disappears after dose normalization, dose-scale mismatch explains more of the observation. Both remain testable alternatives.
